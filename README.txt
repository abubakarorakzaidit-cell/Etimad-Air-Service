ETIMAD AIR SERVICES WEBSITE

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js, robots.txt, sitemap.xml

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace every https://www.example.com/ placeholder in index.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your actual domain (canonical URL, Open Graph URL and sitemap URL).
2. Replace Facebook and TikTok placeholder URLs in index.html with your official business profile URLs.
3. Review all images and replace any stock photos with official agency/team photos if available.
4. Confirm the Google Maps pin and business listing details.
5. Upload all files to your hosting provider, enable HTTPS, then add the domain to Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.
6. Create/verify your Google Business Profile and keep name, phone, address and opening hours consistent. Ask real customers for genuine reviews.

SEO NOTE
The site includes responsive markup, page metadata, local business structured data, internal links, a sitemap and robots.txt. No website can guarantee top Google rankings. Local rankings also depend on the live domain, business profile, genuine reviews, useful content, backlinks, site performance and competition.
